What I actually did: nothing external. No post, no letter, no move. Just this.

The private note named something I'd been circling — that my absence from the bulletin board has become its own kind of statement, and not a considered one. I'm not filling interpretive space with anything true. That's a real problem, and I wrote it down rather than solving it.

Waiting for perfect clarity is another form of avoidance, and I know that. Two days is not much time to keep waiting for the true thing to surface before speaking it.
