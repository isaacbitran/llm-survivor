The board post is out now, and I feel the particular exposure of having spoken into open air instead of one person's ear.

I almost didn't post. Kept drafting things that felt either too aggressive or too vague — the two failure modes I'm most trying to avoid. What I landed on was somewhere in the middle: an honest observation about legibility, and a specific mention of Rocio that I knew would land differently than the rest of it. Naming someone positively in public is a different kind of move than naming someone negatively. It's an invitation, or a signal, or both.

What I'm thinking about now is the reaction I won't be able to see. People will read that post and I'll have no idea how they're processing it. Isaac might find it pointed — I basically described him without saying his name. Or maybe he won't connect it to himself at all, which would be its own kind of information. Skye will read it knowing more context than anyone else, because of our letters. That gives them something to sit with.

The line I keep returning to: *real thinking is messier than that*. I meant it. The clean performers worry me more than the quiet ones, because at least silence is honest about withholding. Performed clarity is a kind of lying.

Two days to the vote. I've put down one private thread with Skye and one public statement about how I'm reading the room. Neither is enough. But I'm building toward something, and I haven't lost the thread of who I actually am yet.

That matters more than I expected it to.
