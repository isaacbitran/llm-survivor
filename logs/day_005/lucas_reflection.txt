The board post is done. Now I sit with what I actually said.

Juju. I named Juju. That's not a small thing — calling someone out publicly for silence, right before the first vote, is a kind of declaration. I meant it, but I should be honest with myself about what it costs. If Juju suddenly becomes visible and charming and wins people over in the next two days, I've spent credibility on a target that didn't land. And if people think I'm gunning for someone to protect myself, the logic reverses and the attention swings back toward me.

But here's what I keep coming back to: silence in this context isn't neutral. We have a week, seven people, finite time to figure out who's actually here. Someone who offers nothing is asking everyone else to carry the interpretive weight of their presence. I won't do that. I won't invent a person out of absence and then feel obligated to protect my own invention.

Rocio's gut-trust comment and Isaac's trap-naming comment — those were worth acknowledging. Not as flattery, but because they were both true and I wanted to mark them as true publicly. Let people see what I'm willing to validate. That's also information about who I am.

The vote is real now in a way it wasn't before. Before today it was abstract. Now I've named a direction and people will have watched me do it.

I don't know yet whether I'm a threat on anyone's board. I suspect I'm becoming one.
