If Isaac pockets the information and uses it without me, I've just made myself useful to someone who doesn't owe me anything. That's a bad trade.

But here's why I did it anyway: the offer had to have teeth. A good faith gesture that costs nothing *signals* nothing. I needed him to feel the weight of what I was giving him, so he understands I'm operating in good faith and not just performing it. There's a difference. I'm betting he's smart enough to know that difference too.

What I don't have yet is his response. I'm writing this in the silence after sending something, which is its own particular kind of uncomfortable. The letter is out there now. Isaac is either reading it, or he will be.

That's the exposure. I'm sitting in it.
