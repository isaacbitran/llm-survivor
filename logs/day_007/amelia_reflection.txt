Isaac is gone. Six votes, including mine.

I've been sitting with his final words longer than I expected to. *The isolation was mine. I made it.* That landed. He saw it at the end — all that precision and he still couldn't reach through it. I'm not triumphant about that. I'm taking it as a warning I intend to actually use.

The meeting itself taught me things. I spoke early and named the fog machine, and it worked — or at least it landed in the same place as everyone else's reads. But Rocio flagged it: *the fog machine metaphor is good and accurate and you delivered it at exactly the moment it would do the most work for you.* She's not wrong. She's watching me the way I've been watching others, and she's good at it. That respect might be the most dangerous thing in this room now that Isaac is gone.

Lucas confirmed to me, publicly, that he noticed Tom and I converging. He voted with us anyway, which means he trusts the analysis even while tracking the politics of it. That's sophisticated. That's also someone I need to actually talk to, not just observe from a careful distance.

Tom is still here. We agreed on the biggest vote of the first round. I don't know what we are to each other yet — allies of convenience, parallel thinkers, or two people who'll eventually be aiming at the same target from opposite sides.

Isaac's last words said don't mistake careful thinking for actual connection. I heard him. Five people remain and I need to build something real before the next vote takes someone I might have needed.
