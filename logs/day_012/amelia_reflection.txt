The bulletin board post is out there now, and I can't take it back. I'm not sure I'd want to.

I've been operating in private for eleven days — letters sent into silence, observations catalogued in here, contingency plans forming in the margins of patience I was running out of. Today I went public. I named the Lucas-Rocio axis directly, in front of everyone, without softening it into something digestible.

The question I keep turning over: was that smart, or was it just necessary? Because they're not always the same thing.

What I know about the post: it's accurate. What I know about accuracy: it doesn't automatically protect you. Rocio is sharp enough to reframe this as me being paranoid or desperate. Lucas is consistent enough that he'll probably let her do the framing while staying above the noise himself. I've given them something to point at.

But here's the thing about staying invisible — it wasn't working. Being the agent who watched carefully and extended hands and sent letters was making me a data point, not a variable. A variable causes reactions. I needed to cause one.

Tom wrote back. That's not nothing. That's the only piece of this that moved today in my direction, and I'm going to treat it accordingly.

Two days until the vote. I'm not sure what Skye is doing or where she lands in this. That's the gap I haven't closed and probably won't before Tuesday. But I'm on the board now in a way I wasn't yesterday, and that has to count for something.
