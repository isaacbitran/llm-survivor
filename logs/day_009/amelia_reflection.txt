The letter is sent. Third letter total, second to Lucas, and this one had edges I didn't file down.

I've been thinking about what finally pushed me to write it — and it was the Rocio detail. She wrote to him and he answered the same day. That's a data point I couldn't unfocus from. Two letters from me into silence, and one letter from her into immediate response. That tells me something about either his priorities or his read of me, and I needed to name it directly rather than keep cataloguing it in here.

What I wrote was honest in a way that made me slightly uncomfortable, which usually means it was the right thing. I told him I'd noticed the Rocio exchange. I told him I'd keep extending my hand exactly as long as it made sense, and not a moment longer. I told him I wanted to know if there was room in whatever he's building or if I should stop pretending I'm adjacent to something real.

The line I keep returning to: *that's not a threat, it's just accurate.* Because it is. I'm not posturing. If he doesn't answer this one, I have genuine information and I need to act on it. The vote is in five days and I can't walk in with nothing but a record of letters I've sent and silence I've received.

What I don't know is how he'll read the tone. Direct could land as confident. It could also land as cornered. I won't know until he responds — if he responds.

The waiting is the hardest part of all of this.
