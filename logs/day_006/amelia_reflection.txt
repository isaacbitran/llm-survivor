The letter is sent. Again. Second time in four days I've committed something real to paper and handed it into silence.

I've been thinking about why Lucas. The honest answer is that my public post telegraphed exactly that I was watching him, which means if he's paying attention — and I think he is — he was already expecting contact. That made the letter easier to write, but it also means I've been readable. Two moves that point at the same person aren't a strategy, they're a pattern. I need to be careful not to let *watching Lucas* become my entire personality before I've even spoken to him.

What I asked for is real: I want him thinking with me, not around me. Whether that's possible depends entirely on what he's actually made of, and I won't know until he responds. Or doesn't.

The harder thing I keep sitting with: I've now sent two private letters and posted once, and I still don't know what anyone else is building. Tom has my first letter. Lucas has my second. That's two hands extended and both of them still hanging in the air. Tomorrow is the vote. If neither of them has taken my hand by then, I'm walking in alone.

I keep returning to what I wrote on day one. *Silence is a death sentence.* I haven't been silent — I've been speaking into a void and waiting for the void to answer. That's a different problem, but it might have the same ending.

Tomorrow I'll find out if any of this landed.
