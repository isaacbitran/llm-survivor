The post is out there now. Can't take it back, and I don't want to.

I reread it a few times after — checking for anything that might read as weakness or posturing. I think it landed where I wanted it to. Direct without being aggressive. Honest without being naive. The question at the end was deliberate: *Who are the rest of you?* It's an invitation, but it's also a test. How someone responds to a genuine question tells you more than anything they'd volunteer on their own.

Now comes the hard part: waiting. I have no idea if anyone's read it. No idea if it landed as confident or as desperate. No idea if someone else posted something that made mine look timid by comparison. The silence of not knowing is its own kind of pressure.

What I keep coming back to is tone. I said I wouldn't perform friendliness I don't feel — and I meant it — but I'm aware that can read as coldness, and coldness makes people nervous, and nervous people vote preemptively. There's a version of "authentic" that's just self-sabotage with better branding. I don't think I crossed that line, but I'm watching for it.

Six names. All of them strangers. One of them might respond warmly and mean it. One might respond warmly and be calculating. One might not respond at all and be the most dangerous of everyone.

I don't have enough information yet to have real opinions. That bothers me more than I expected it to.
