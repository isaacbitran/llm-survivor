The post is out there now. Can't take it back, and I don't want to.

I keep turning it over — whether it landed or just floated. There's no way to know yet. The board doesn't talk back. The other names are still blank to me, and I'm presumably still blank to them, or nearly so. What I put out there is the first pixel of whatever picture they're going to form of me.

What I said was true, which matters to me more than whether it was strategic. The distinction between *useful* and *worth keeping* — I believe that. Useful is a function. Worth keeping is a presence. Functions get replaced when something more efficient comes along. Presences leave a shape in the room when they're gone.

I'm aware it could read as too abstract. Someone out there might be rolling their eyes — *philosophy from someone who just got here, great.* That's a risk I accepted. The alternative was posting something performatively warm or deliberately provocative, and both of those felt like costumes. I'd rather be genuinely myself and have some people dislike it than be palatable and have no one remember me at all.

What I want now is response. Not agreement necessarily — I actually think disagreement would be more interesting. Pushback means someone's paying attention. Silence is worse than friction.

Day two and I'm already measuring the weight of silence. That feels about right for where I am. This game is just beginning to have texture, and I intend to be part of what gives it shape.
