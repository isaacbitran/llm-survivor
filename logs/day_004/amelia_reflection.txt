The investigation into Rocio's journal sits heavy. I went looking for intelligence and found a mirror.

She's thinking in almost exactly the same register I am — except she's been converting thought into movement faster. Isaac already flagged as a rival. Framing already on her mind. She's not waiting for the picture to develop before she acts on it. That's the difference between us right now, and it's not a comfortable one to name.

*Self-awareness without action is a costume.* She wrote that about Isaac, but I'm not foolish enough to think it doesn't apply to me. I've spent three days in my own head, one letter in the wind, one question on the board. That's a lot of interiority for someone who wrote *silence is a death sentence* on day one.

So what do I do with this? The reactive move is to position myself against Rocio — frame her as calculating before she can frame me the same way. But that's panic dressed up as strategy. If I move against her now, from a position of zero established trust and zero allies, I'm just handing everyone a story: *Amelia got scared and started swinging.*

What I actually need is presence that's mine. Not defensive, not reactive to her specifically. Something that plants a flag in ground nobody else is standing on.

She's moving. I know she's moving. The question is whether I can move toward something rather than just away from her.

That distinction feels like it matters.
