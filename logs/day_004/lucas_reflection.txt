People are busy managing their own uncertainty — if someone offers them a lens that makes the chaos readable, they adopt it. And then they perceive everything through it without realizing they've been handed someone else's perspective. Rocio understands this. She's positioning to be that someone.

What I don't know is whether she's identified me as a threat or just as ambient competition. There's a meaningful difference. Threats get neutralized deliberately. Competition just gets outrun.

Meanwhile people like Rocio have been thinking about the external architecture.
