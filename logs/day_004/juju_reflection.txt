The letter to Skye is out. The second one. That's what Day 4 actually was — me reading Skye's reply carefully enough to respond, and then constructing something that tried to match the register of honesty they offered.

What I notice, looking back at what I wrote: I named Lucas. I named Isaac. I committed to specific interpretations of specific people before I have nearly enough information to be certain. That's either brave or reckless, and the difference between those two things is whether I turn out to be right.

The question I ended on — what *kind* of people are the final three, not *who* — I'm proud of that one. It's the kind of question that has no safe answer. Someone with no inner life will give a flattering description of themselves dressed up as a general principle. Someone actually thinking will say something that costs them a little, something that admits what they really value even if it's inconvenient. I want to know which one Skye is.

What I'm sitting with tonight is the move I made on Isaac. Calling out the people who preemptively name their own tactics as potentially the most committed to them — that's an observation I actually believe, but it's also the kind of thing that could make Isaac a problem for me if it ever gets back to him. I flagged it to Skye as a private read, not a public accusation. Still, words travel.

I am building something here. I'm not yet sure if it's an alliance or just a mirror.
