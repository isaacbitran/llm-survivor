I wrote to Skye today. Finally. The gap in my map is smaller now, or at least I put something into it.

What I said was honest: Lucas has no anchor, Juju and Amelia are a structure whether or not they call it one, and Skye and I are the two least committed to any existing line. I gave her the only credential I have — that I kept my word to Amelia — and asked her what she sees.

Now I wait. That's the uncomfortable part. I put something real into the world and I have no idea what comes back.

I've been thinking about why it took me this long to write to her. Part of it was the compression. Every day feels like it's moving faster than the last, and I kept telling myself I'd understand her better after one more observation. But that's not discipline, that's avoidance dressed up as patience. The gap was a gap because I let it stay one.

What I actually believe going into this: if Skye has been watching clearly, she already knows what I know about the shape of the four of us. She doesn't need me to explain it to her. What she needs to know is whether I'm someone worth moving with or someone performing the motion of moving. The letter was an attempt to be the former.

I don't know if it landed that way. I won't know until she writes back.

The vote is coming and I still don't fully know who Skye is. But she knows I asked.
