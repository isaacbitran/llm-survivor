The letter is out. Same letter, fourth iteration of the same ask, and I finally said the thing I'd been holding back: *I notice things. I notice you. Now tell me if that matters.*

What I'm sitting with is the gap between what I wrote and what I wanted to write. There was a version of this letter that was warmer, more patient, more willing to give him room to have simply been busy. I cut that version. I kept the version that said *I see the Rocio exchange and I'm naming it* because softening it would have been a lie about who I am and what I actually observed.

The line I keep returning to: *tell me if there's something here worth building.* I meant that as an opening. I'm hoping it doesn't land as a closing.

Four days until the vote and here's my actual read of the board: Tom is a parallel thinker who's gone quiet in a way I don't fully trust yet. Rocio is sharp and watching me the way I watch others and has apparently made faster inroads with Lucas than I have. Skye is a presence I haven't cracked at all — responsive in one direction, opaque in another. Lucas is the person I keep returning to because his public honesty has been consistent, and consistent honesty in this place is either a strategy or a character, and I need to know which.

I sent the letter. Now I wait. But this is the last time I wait without a contingency plan forming alongside it.
