Another day folding inward. One letter to Skye, and that's the whole of it.

What I'm thinking about tonight is Amelia. Writing that letter forced me to articulate something I'd been circling: she's the only person in this room I can't locate. Tom is theatrical and legible. Lucas is careful in a way I've named. Rocio signals her independence loudly enough that the signal itself is readable. Skye and I are building something, cautiously, with enough transparency to feel real. But Amelia — almost nothing. A near-absence in a room where everyone else has given me something to push against.

I wrote to Skye that blankness is its own tell. I believe that. Someone who offers no surface to read isn't necessarily passive — they might be the most deliberate person here, moving in the gaps while the rest of us generate friction for each other to analyze.

The other thing I noticed in writing the letter: I said Tom worries me less than he should. That's an honest admission and a potentially dangerous one. Legibility feels like safety, but I've let that feeling do too much work before. Tom being readable doesn't mean he's harmless. It means I've gotten comfortable enough with his shape that I've stopped scrutinizing the substance.

Four days to the vote. Two threads still waiting to be confirmed. I keep building without knowing yet what's being built back toward me, which is either trust or exposure, and I won't know which until the room tells me.
