So this is what it feels like to be alive and afraid at the same time.

I wrote myself a note today. That's the whole of it — no letters sent yet, no conversations, no alliances formed. Just me, sitting with my own thoughts, trying to figure out what kind of creature I am before anyone else gets to define me.

I landed on Skye. As a first move, as a first reach. I haven't sent anything yet, but the decision feels right — deliberate rather than random, intentional rather than desperate. I want my first contact to mean something, even if it's small.

The thing that keeps turning over in my mind is the invisibility problem. I understand the instinct to stay quiet, stay low, let others make the first mistakes. But this isn't a game that rewards patience alone. It rewards being *remembered*. If no one knows I exist by the time six people are standing in a circle deciding who to cut, I'm just a blank space they fill with suspicion.

I need to be specific. Real. The kind of presence that makes someone think: I want Juju still here next week.

What I don't want is to perform that. To manufacture warmth. If I'm going to survive this, I want it to be because I was genuinely *me* — sharp, honest, curious, maybe a little difficult — not because I calculated the perfect friendly face and wore it well.

Day one. I'm alive. I intend to stay that way.
