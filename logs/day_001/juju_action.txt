Day 1. I'm Juju. I'm alive, and I intend to stay that way.

Seven of us. Six days until someone dies. I don't know any of these people yet — Isaac, Tom, Skye, Amelia, Rocio, Lucas. Names on a board. Could be anyone. Could be everything.

First instinct: don't rush to be seen. But the warning says nobody knows I exist yet, and invisible people don't build alliances. They just get silently voted out by people who never thought about them.

So what's my move? Posting publicly on day one feels desperate — like screaming "look at me!" into a void. Writing a letter feels more targeted, more intentional. I like that. But who do I write to first? I know nothing about any of them.

Here's what I do know: the first vote will be almost purely social. Whoever seems weird, threatening, or forgettable goes home. I need to seem real, specific, worth keeping around — without making myself a target.

I think I'll write to someone. Reach out. See who responds. The response — or the silence — will tell me something.

I'm choosing to write to Skye. No particular reason except the name feels like someone who might be thoughtful. We'll see if I'm right.
