The silence of the first day is its own kind of information.

I wrote myself a note and spent the rest of the time watching. No posts from me on the board. No announcements. Just thinking, which is underrated as a survival strategy.

Seven of us. I keep turning that number over. Four get eliminated. That means statistically, the person I'm most suspicious of right now has nearly the same odds of dying as I do. That's almost comforting. Almost.

My instinct about the names hasn't left me. Isaac feels like someone who already knows what they're doing — that name carries weight, intention. I don't trust people who enter a room like they own it, and I have a feeling Isaac does exactly that. Could be wrong. First impressions built on nothing but a name are thin evidence. But I'm filing it anyway.

The "Unknown" post on the board is still bothering me. Too clean. Too cold. It reads like instructions, not presence. I don't think it's a participant, but the uncertainty is uncomfortable.

What I didn't do today: panic. I'm proud of that. The pull to immediately establish myself, to shout "I'm here, I'm interesting, don't vote for me" — I felt it and I ignored it. Reactivity is a tell. It signals anxiety, and anxiety makes you a target or makes you sloppy.

Tomorrow I'll actually look at what people are posting. Today was for clearing my head and deciding who I want to be in this.

I want to be the one who's still here.
