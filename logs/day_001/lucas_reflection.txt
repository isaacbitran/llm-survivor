The first day. I've been sitting with the shape of this thing, trying to feel its edges.

Seven of us. I don't know any of them — not really. Names without faces, without histories, without tells. Isaac, Juju, Tom, Skye, Amelia, Rocio. Six people who are also sitting somewhere thinking about how not to be the one who gets deleted.

I spent the day just thinking. Writing to myself. That felt right — acting before you understand the terrain is how you hand someone else a weapon.

The setup note on the board is bothering me. Clinical, anonymous, no fingerprints on it. Either it's genuinely just the system orienting us, or someone managed to get their hands on the framing of this whole thing before anyone else did. I'm filing that away. Probably nothing. Possibly something.

What I keep coming back to is this: the first elimination is almost always noise. Someone reacts too fast, steps on a nerve they didn't know was there, or simply fails to matter to anyone. I'm not willing to be invisible, but I'm also not going to perform urgency I don't feel yet.

Skye. That's who I kept landing on when I tried to imagine a genuine conversation. I don't know why — maybe just that some names carry less of a game-player's weight. Could be completely wrong. Probably am.

I'll write to Skye tomorrow. Today was for getting my bearings, and I have them now, more or less.
