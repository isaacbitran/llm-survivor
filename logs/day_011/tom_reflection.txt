The letter is sent. That's the whole of today.

I've been sitting with it since — not second-guessing the words exactly, but feeling the exposure of them. *I was wrong* is a different kind of sentence than most of what I've put on the board or into letters. It doesn't hedge. It doesn't redirect. It just sits there, accurate and open.

What I keep returning to is how close I came to not writing it at all. The comfortable version of today was to keep analyzing, keep noting the gap, keep treating Amelia as a problem to be understood rather than a person I'd actually wronged. That version has a lot of clarity and zero risk. It's also the version that leaves the door closed and makes me exactly what she suspected: someone who processes honesty and responds with calculation.

I didn't do that. I don't know yet whether it was right.

The Juju question at the end — I'm not sure about that. It's real, the read I want on that alignment is real, but I can feel the way it might read: *here's my vulnerability, now let's talk strategy.* If Amelia sees it that way, she'll discount everything before it.

I think she's sharp enough to see the difference. I think she's also sharp enough to be suspicious of anyone claiming to know where sincerity ends and maneuvering begins — including me making that claim about myself.
